# Graphs

**Menu:** ALF > Content Creation > Graphs

Turns a **CSV file** (from Excel, LibreOffice, Google Sheets or lab software) or a set of **formulas** into a chart on a board in the room, readable in VR and AR. You can make a **bar chart**, **line chart**, **scatter plot**, **box plot** or **histogram**.

In the lesson, learners can grab and move the graph, resize it with both hands, zoom in and point at a value to read it exactly. You can switch each of these off.

## Quick start

1. Open **ALF > Content Creation > Graphs**.
2. In step **1 Data**, press **Choose a file…** and pick a CSV file, or switch to **Formulas** and type one. Check the preview, then press **Import and add the graph to the scene** (or **Compute and add the graph to the scene**).
3. In step **2 Chart**, check the kind of chart, the columns drawn and the titles.
4. In step **3 Appearance**, set the size, theme and anything else you want to show.
5. In step **4 Placement**, choose where the graph appears and what learners can do with it.
6. In step **5 Save**, press **Save the scene**.
