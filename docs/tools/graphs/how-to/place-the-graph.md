# Place the graph and choose what learners can do

**Goal:** decide where the graph appears and how learners can use it, in step **4 Placement**.

## Where it appears

| Option | What happens |
|---|---|
| **In the world** | The graph stands where you put it, at the **position** and **rotation** you set. |
| **In front of the learner** | Each time the graph is shown, it appears at eye level at the **Distance from the learner (m)** you set, facing them. It moves along when they walk or teleport. About 1.5 m suits a graph of 1.2 m by 0.8 m. |

## What learners can do

All of these are on for a new graph:

| Option | What learners can do |
|---|---|
| **Learners can grab and move it** | Grab the graph and put it somewhere else. It straightens itself if let go slightly tilted. A **Lock in place** button next to the board fixes it. |
| **Learners can resize it with both hands** | Grab it with both hands and pull apart or together. Texts stay sharp at the new size. |
| **Learners can zoom in** | Use **+**, **–** and **Show all** next to the board, and drag the zoomed view. Line charts and scatter plots only. |
| **Learners see a value when they point at it** | Point at a bar, point or box to see its exact value. |

The graph also **turns towards the learner** when they walk around it. A **Stop turning** button next to the board switches that off.

## Good to know

- **Show the graph in one lesson step only** by switching it on in that step with [Scenario Setup](../../scenario-setup/how-to/show-or-hide-objects.md). Placed **in front of the learner**, it then appears right where they are.
