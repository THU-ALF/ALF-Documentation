# Exploded View

**Menu:** ALF > Interactivity > Exploded View Setup

Pulls a machine apart so learners can see every piece, then puts it back together. It can open when the lesson reaches a certain step, when a learner presses a button, or both. Learners can also pick up the pieces and look at them closely.

:::warning[Your model must be made of separate pieces]
The tool moves the pieces of a model; it can't cut a single solid model apart. See [Check your model first](./how-to/before-you-start.md#check-your-model-first).
:::

## Quick start

1. Open **ALF > Interactivity > Exploded View Setup**.
2. **1 Model:** drag your model from the **Hierarchy** into step 1.
3. **2 Coming apart:** the distances are filled in for you.
4. **3 Try it out:** press the large button to see the model come apart.
5. **4 Handling:** choose whether learners can pick up the pieces.
6. **5 Trigger:** choose a lesson step, a push button, or both.
7. **6 Save:** press **Save into the scene**, then save the scene with **Ctrl+S**.
