# Use the preview

The preview shows the model on a fixed plate, so you can judge its size and how it stands.

## Look around

- **Drag** in the preview to orbit around the model.
- **Scroll** to zoom in and out. This only changes the view, not the model's size.

## Buttons and options below the preview

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Comparison view** | Goes back to the standard view. It's the same for every model, so you can compare their sizes. |
| **Find model** | Zooms in on the model, for example when it's very small. Doesn't change the model. |
| **Center on origin** | Moves the model back to the middle of the plate and sets it on the floor. Size and rotation stay the same. Can be undone with **Ctrl+Z**. |
| **1 m ruler** | Shows a 1 metre ruler with 10 cm marks. |
| **1.75 m human** | Shows the outline of a person 1.75 m tall, to check the size at a glance. |
| **Highlight model** | Outlines the model in cyan, so small models are easy to spot. |

The ruler, the person and the plate are only for checking. They're never saved with the model.

## Adjust in the Scene view instead

Tick **Adjust in Scene view (show existing scene)** to work on the model in Unity's Scene view, next to the rest of your scene. This helps to compare its size with other objects in the room.

- Use **W** to move, **E** to turn and **R** to resize, or keep using the fields in step 2.
- The model is only a preview. It isn't added to your scene and doesn't appear in the Hierarchy.

Untick the box to go back to the plate.

## Warnings

If the model is outside the view, off the plate or below the floor, the tool tells you. Use **Find model** to locate it, or **Center on origin** to bring it back.
