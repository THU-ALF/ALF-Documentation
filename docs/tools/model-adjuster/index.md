# Model Adjuster

**Menu:** ALF > Model Adjuster

Prepares a 3D model before you place it with the [Object Spawner](../object-spawner/index.md). Models from other programs often arrive lying on their side, far too big or small, or floating below the floor. Model Adjuster lets you turn, resize and set the model down on a preview plate, then saves it as a **new prefab** that sits upright and at the right size. The original model is never changed.

## Quick start

1. Open **ALF > Model Adjuster**.
2. In step **1 Model**, choose the model in **Model asset** and press **Load model into preview**.
3. In step **2 Adjust**, set the **Position**, **Rotation** and **Scale multiplier** until it looks right.
4. In step **3 Corrections**, tick **Snap to ground** and/or **Snap rotation to 90°**, press **Apply selected corrections**, then **I like this position, orientation and size**.
5. In step **4 Save**, press **Save adjusted prefab…** and choose a name.

The Object Spawner then opens with your new prefab, ready to place.
