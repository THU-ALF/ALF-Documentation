# The exercises

Each exercise (module) practises one kind of control. They run in this order.

| Exercise | Learners practise | Ticked when |
|---|---|---|
| **Buttons** | Pressing a button by touching it, and pressing a button further away by pointing at it. If your lesson has [clickable parts](../../button-injection/how-to/make-a-part-clickable.md), also selecting the lid of a box. | Always included |
| **Grab and place** | Picking up a cube and putting it on a plate. If your lesson has objects learners resize, also making the cube bigger with both hands. | The lesson has objects to pick up |
| **Machine controls** | Pressing **Positive** on a control panel until a sliding block stops at its end, as with machines made with the [Kinematic Editor Tool](../../xr-kinematics/index.md). | The lesson has moving machine parts |
| **Sliders** | Dragging a slider until a panel shows 80 %. | The lesson has sliders, for example in the [Planetary Lab](../../planetary-lab/index.md) or a [Motion](../../motion/index.md) playback |
| **Moving around** | Getting to a marked spot on the floor: by teleporting, moving by hand gesture or walking, whatever the player setup allows. With controllers that can turn, also turning with the thumbstick. | Lesson steps ask learners to go somewhere |
| **Wrist menu** | Turning the left wrist until the wrist menu appears, and holding it there. | A lesson step uses the wrist menu |
| **QR positioning** | Looking at the printed QR code until the model locks into place. AR only. | The lesson uses [Positioning Setup](../../positioning/index.md) |
| **Real surfaces** | Putting a block down on a real table, seen through the headset. AR only. | The lesson uses the room's real surfaces |

You can tick or untick any exercise that fits the player setup. Exercises marked **Coming later** aren't available yet.

## Which exercises fit which setup

| Exercise | VR | AR | Player setup unknown |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buttons, Grab and place, Machine controls, Sliders | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ instructions name both hands and controllers |
| Moving around | ✓ if the player can teleport or move | ✓ by walking | ✗ |
| Wrist menu | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ |
| QR positioning | ✗ | ✓ with QR codes in the lesson | ✗ |
| Real surfaces | ✗ | ✓ with a scanned room | ✗ |

In VR, Grab and place adds a small practice table. In AR it doesn't: the learner's own room is the surface.

## Controls on controllers and hands

| To… | With controllers | With hands |
|---|---|---|
| Press a near button | Push it with the controller's tip | Push it with a fingertip |
| Press a far button | Point and pull the **trigger** | Point and pinch |
| Select a clickable part | Point and press the **grip** | Point and pinch |
| Grab | Press the **grip** | Pinch |
