# Change or remove the warm-up

## Update after changing the lesson

When you add or change lesson content, for example new buttons or objects to pick up:

1. Open **ALF > XR Pre-Training**.
2. In step **2 Modules**, check the ticked exercises. Newly used skills may now be ticked.
3. In step **3 Generate**, press **Regenerate the warm-up**.
4. Save the scene.

Regenerating keeps what you moved or changed in exercises that stay ticked. Newly ticked exercises are added, unticked ones removed.

## Change the language or the hint delay

Change **Language** in step 2 or **Hint after (seconds)** in step 3, then press **Regenerate the warm-up**. The new setting only applies after regenerating.

## Move the station

If the practice objects overlap your lesson's content, or stand in an awkward place:

1. In step **3 Generate**, press **Select the station**. It's selected in the Hierarchy and shown in the Scene view.
2. Move it in the Scene view. The practice objects, the panel and the hint move with it.
3. Save the scene.

In the editor, all practice objects are visible at once and overlap each other. That's expected: in the lesson, each exercise's objects only appear during its own steps.

## Remove the warm-up

1. In step **3 Generate**, press **Remove the warm-up** and confirm with **Yes, remove**.
2. Save the scene.

The practice steps and the station are removed. Your lesson steps are left exactly as they were. **Ctrl+Z** brings the warm-up back.
