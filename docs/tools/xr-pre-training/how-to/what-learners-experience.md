# What learners experience

## The order

1. A **welcome** step explains the warm-up. Learners press **Next** on the panel.
2. The **exercises** follow, one skill per step. Only the objects for the current exercise are visible, the rest are hidden.
3. A **closing** step says the lesson starts now. Learners press **Next**, and the practice station disappears.
4. Your **lesson** begins.

The instruction panel stands to the left of the practice objects. A short feedback line appears below it.

## Feedback

- **When they get it right**, the practice object briefly turns green and the feedback line shows "✓ Well done!" (in German: "✓ Gut gemacht!"). The next step starts by itself.
- **When they're stuck** for longer than the hint delay (15 seconds by default), the feedback line gives a more explicit hint, for example where the button is and how to press it, and the object pulses amber.

## Good to know

- **Next doesn't skip an exercise.** On steps that check what the learner does, Next does nothing until the task is done. The instruction and the hint say what to do.
- **Learners can go back** from the lesson's first step into the warm-up with **Previous**, to practise again.
- **The canvas button is labelled "Next"** in both languages, so the German instructions say „Next“ too.
