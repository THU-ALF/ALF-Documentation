# XR Pre-Training

**Menu:** ALF > XR Pre-Training

Adds a short **warm-up** in front of a lesson. Before the content starts, learners practise the XR controls the lesson uses with their own hands: pressing buttons, pointing, grabbing, moving around. When the lesson begins, they can focus on its content instead of on how the headset works.

You don't build the warm-up by hand. The tool **looks at your lesson**, suggests the exercises it needs, and generates them: a practice station in front of the learner and practice steps that run before your lesson's own steps. Your lesson steps stay exactly as they are.

## Quick start

1. Build the lesson with [Scenario Setup](../scenario-setup/index.md) first.
2. Open **ALF > XR Pre-Training**.
3. In step **1 Lesson**, check that the tool found your lesson and the player setup.
4. In step **2 Modules**, check the exercises it ticked, and choose the **Language**.
5. In step **3 Generate**, press **Add the warm-up**, then **Save the scene**.
