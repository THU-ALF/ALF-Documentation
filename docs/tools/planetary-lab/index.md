# Planetary Lab

**Menu:** ALF > Physics > Planetary Lab Setup

Builds a **physics experiment** in the room: two objects, such as a feather and a metal block, are dropped side by side. Learners change the gravity, the air density and an object's mass, for example to compare Earth, the Moon and Mars or to switch to a vacuum, and watch how the fall changes.

The lab comes with:

- **controls** for learners to change the conditions,
- **graphs** of each object's height and speed during the fall,
- **arrows** showing the forces: weight, air resistance and buoyancy,
- an optional **guided lesson** in English and German.

## Quick start

1. Make a lesson scene with [Scene Generation](../scene-generation/index.md).
2. Open **ALF > Physics > Planetary Lab Setup**.
3. In step **1 System**, press **Create a lab in the active scene**.
4. Work through steps **2 Bodies** to **6 Lesson**.
5. In step **7 Save**, press **Apply lab settings**, then save the scene with **Ctrl+S**.
