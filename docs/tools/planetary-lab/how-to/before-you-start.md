# What learners see and do

New to Unity? The basic terms (Scene, Hierarchy, Project window, Play mode) are explained in [Before you start](../../../before-you-start.md).

## What you need

A **lesson scene** with a room and a player setup, made with [Scene Generation](../../scene-generation/index.md). The Planetary Lab doesn't set those up itself.

## What learners see

Two objects stand side by side on a platform. When learners release them, both fall at the same moment. They can:

- **release** the objects and watch them fall,
- **reset** them to their starting position,
- **explore**: pick the objects up and throw them,
- change the **gravity**, the **air density** and the **mass** of one object, or choose a ready-made environment such as Earth, the Moon or Mars,
- read the **height and speed graphs** of each fall, and see the **force arrows**.

## Environments and objects

The lab uses ready-made **profiles**:

- **Environments** such as Earth, the Moon and Mars, each with its gravity and air density.
- **Objects** such as a light and a heavy sphere, a feather and a metal block, each with its mass, size and air resistance.

:::note[The values are illustrative]
The air resistance values are reasonable estimates for teaching, not measured from real objects. A feather is shown as a simple shape, so its fluttering isn't simulated.
:::

## The window

| Step | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **1 System** | Create the lab, and choose the starting environment and the environments learners can pick. |
| **2 Bodies** | Choose the two objects, their shape and size. |
| **3 Placement** | Where the lab stands, and how high the objects start. |
| **4 Controls** | The ranges learners can set. |
| **5 Measurements** | Run time and what counts as a finished drop. |
| **6 Lesson** | Optional guided lesson in English and German. |
| **7 Save** | Apply the settings, or remove the lab. |

Each scene can have **one** lab.
