# Update or remove a lab

## Change the lab

1. Open the scene and **ALF > Physics > Planetary Lab Setup**. The scene's lab is chosen automatically.
2. Change what you need in steps **1** to **6**.
3. In step **7 Save**, press **Apply lab settings**. The lab is updated. Its objects, controls and graphs are kept, and so is everything else in your scene.
4. Save the scene with **Ctrl+S**.

## Remove the lab

In step **7 Save**, press **Remove generated lab**. Only what the tool created is removed. Your own objects and the rest of the scene stay.

**Ctrl+Z** brings it back.

## Good to know

- **The learners' drops are never saved** in the project. Each lesson starts fresh.
- **A warning at the bottom of the window** tells you if a setting doesn't fit, for example a range that doesn't include one of the environments.
