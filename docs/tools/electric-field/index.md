# Electric Field

**Menu:** ALF > Physics > Electric Field Setup

Builds an **electrostatics experiment** in the room: one to four point charges float in front of the learner, with the electric field around them shown as **arrows** and **field lines**. Learners move a **test probe** through the field to read the field strength and the force on a test charge, and run a **scan** that plots both along a straight path.

The lab comes with:

- ready-made **arrangements**: a single charge, two like charges, and a dipole;
- a **test probe** with live readouts of the field E (N/C) and the force F (N);
- a **scan** that draws a graph of |E| and |F| along a path;
- controls for the arrows, the field lines and the arrow scale;
- an optional **guided lesson** in English and German.

It's the sibling of the [Planetary Lab](../planetary-lab/index.md) and works the same way.

## Quick start

1. Make a lesson scene with [Scene Generation](../scene-generation/index.md).
2. Open **ALF > Physics > Electric Field Setup**.
3. In step **1 Lab**, press **Create lab in active scene**.
4. Work through steps **2 Charges** to **5 Lesson**.
5. In step **6 Save**, press **Apply lab settings**, then save the scene with **Ctrl+S**.
