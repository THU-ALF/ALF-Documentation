# Update or remove a lab

## Change the lab

1. Open the scene and **ALF > Physics > Electric Field Setup**. The scene's lab is chosen automatically. If not, pick it under **Lab to edit** in step 1.
2. Change what you need in steps **1** to **5**.
3. In step **6 Save**, press **Apply lab settings**. The lab's controls, arrows and lines are rebuilt. The charges, the probe and the graph are kept, and so is everything else in your scene.
4. Save the scene with **Ctrl+S**.

## Remove the lab

In step **6 Save**, press **Remove generated lab (Undo supported)**. Only what the tool created is removed. Your own objects and the rest of the scene stay.

**Ctrl+Z** brings it back.

## Good to know

- **Learners' scans are never saved** in the project. Each lesson starts fresh.
- **Changed the number of charges?** Check the lesson's rules in step 5, then apply the lesson again.
