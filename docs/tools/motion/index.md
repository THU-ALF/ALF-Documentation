# Motion

**Menu:** ALF > Content Creation > Motion > **Demonstration Recorder** and **Scenario Authoring**

Lets you **show** learners how to operate a machine. You record your hand movements and your spoken explanation on a **Meta Quest 3** with hand tracking. Learners then watch see-through **ghost hands** repeat exactly what you did, while the machine reacts and your voice explains each step.

The tool has two parts, opened from two menu entries:

| Part | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Demonstration Recorder** | Record your hands and voice while you operate the machine. |
| **Scenario Authoring** | Turn the recording into a playback station that learners watch in the lesson. |

## Quick start

1. Open **ALF > Content Creation > Motion > Demonstration Recorder**.
2. Choose the machine, and press **Inject Recording Rig into Scene**.
3. Connect the Quest 3 with Quest Link, press **Play**, and record with the menu on your wrist.
4. Open **Scenario Authoring**, choose the machine and your recording, and press **Load & Inspect Motion Clip**.
5. Check the preview, then press **Generate Learning Scenario Station** and **Save Open Scenes**.
