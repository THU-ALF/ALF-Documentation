# Object Spawner

**Menu:** ALF > Interactivity > Object Spawner

Puts any model into your lesson, for example a tool, a spare part or a piece of equipment, and decides what learners can do with it: pick it up, poke it, resize it, or let it fall.

The model can stand **in the room** or sit **on another object** and move with it.

## Quick start

1. Open **ALF > Interactivity > Object Spawner**.
2. In step **1 Object**, drag a model from the Project window into **Prefab or model**, choose where it goes, and press **Place it in the world** or **Place it on this object**.
3. In step **2 Position**, move it into place.
4. In step **3 Interaction**, tick what learners can do with it.
5. In step **4 Save**, press **Save the scene**.
