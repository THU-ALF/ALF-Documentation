# Add, reorder or remove steps

**Goal:** change the number and order of steps in your lesson.

## Add a step

- **At the end:** in tab **2 Steps**, press **Add New Step** under the list.
- **Several at once:** in tab **1 System**, raise **Number of Steps** and press **Update Count**. New steps are added at the end.

## Move a step

1. In tab **2 Steps**, click the step in the list.
2. Use **▲** to move it one place earlier, or **▼** to move it one place later.

## Remove a step

1. In tab **2 Steps**, click the step in the list.
2. Press **Remove**.

## Then generate

Changes to the steps only reach the scene when you press **Update Existing Scenario System** in tab **3 Generate**. Save the scene afterwards with **Ctrl+S**.

## Good to know

- **A lesson has 1 to 20 steps.**
- **The first and last steps are special.** The first is marked **Initial** and the last **End**. If you move a step to the start or end, it becomes the first or last step.
- **Lowering Number of Steps removes steps from the end.** Check that you don't lose steps you need.
