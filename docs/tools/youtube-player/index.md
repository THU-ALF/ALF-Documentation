# YouTube Player

**Menu:** ALF > Content Creation > YouTube Player Setup

Adds a **video screen** that plays a YouTube video and stays in the room for the whole lesson, for example an introduction video by the entrance, or a how-to video next to a machine.

The screen shows only the video, without YouTube's own buttons. Learners use the screen's controls: **Play**, **Pause**, **Restart**, **Mute** and a progress bar to jump to another point in the video. A video never starts by itself: learners press the large **Play video** button over the picture first.

:::tip[Video for one lesson step?]
For a video that belongs to one step of a lesson, use the video option in [Scenario Setup](../scenario-setup/how-to/write-the-instructions.md#video) or Guidance Setup instead. This tool is for a screen that's always there.
:::

## Quick start

1. Open **ALF > Content Creation > YouTube Player Setup**.
2. Give the screen a **Name**, for example *Intro video*.
3. Paste the link into **YouTube URL**. Check that the video ID appears and that the check below says the video **can be embedded**.
4. Choose the **Player Size** and set the **Spawn Coordinates**.
5. Press **Generate YouTube Player**.
6. Save the scene with **Ctrl+S**.

The video plays only in the app on the headset, not in the Unity Editor.
