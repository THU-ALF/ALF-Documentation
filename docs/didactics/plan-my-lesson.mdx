# Plan my lesson

The tool guides show you **how** to build a lesson. This page helps you decide **what** to build, before you open Unity.

Answer eight short questions about your goal, your learners and your equipment. You get:

- **your stages:** which parts of the lesson happen in VR, in AR and on the real object;
- **your learning path:** the order of steps that suits your goal;
- **tips that fit your answers,** each with the reason it works and a link to the ALF guide that shows how;
- **a checklist** to go through before learners try the lesson.

:::tip[Your plan has its own link]
Your answers are stored in the page address. Copy the link to come back later or to share the plan with a colleague. Nothing is sent anywhere.
:::

## How to use the plan

1. **Write the goal sentence** first, on paper. The planner assumes you have one.
2. **Plan the steps on paper** for each stage: what the learner reads, does and sees. See [Scenario Setup: Plan the lesson first](../tools/scenario-setup/how-to/before-you-start.md#plan-the-lesson-first).
3. **Build** with the tools, in the order of the [introduction](../intro.mdx#building-a-lesson-in-order).
4. **Try it yourself** in the headset, then with one learner, and go through the checklist.

:::note[A planner, not a rulebook]
The tips are good defaults from learning research. You know your subject and your learners: if a tip doesn't fit, leave it out. [The theory behind the guidelines](./theory.md) explains where each recommendation comes from.
:::
